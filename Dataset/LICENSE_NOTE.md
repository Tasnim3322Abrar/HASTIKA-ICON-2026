The HASTIKA dataset is provided for **research and non-commercial use** only, as part of the
HASTIKA @ ICON-2026 shared task. By downloading you agree not to redistribute the data, not to
attempt to de-anonymise or contact any individual, and not to use it to target or harm any
individual or community. The data contains hateful content collected from public social media
for research purposes and does not reflect the organisers' views.
